This portfolio is organized as a timeline. Each project records the problem, the constraints, the reasoning behind the design decisions, and how the result was validated. The through line is a progression in liquid propulsion, from component design, to owning the feed system on a vehicle that flew, to leading full-system architecture on a cryogenic stand. Alongside the hardware are the analysis tools I built for my teams to design against, and a professional R&D internship that applied the same discipline, defining what good enough means and proving it, to a sourcing decision rather than a rocket.

    2026 - Present active

    Project Turbopump

    Electric-drive propellant pumps, from first principles to first prints

    Sole designer · Personal project
